About

A slow index in a fast feed.

DDN collects designers, artists, studios and visual practices worth spending more than a few seconds with. A new feature is published once a week. Profiles are based on primary sources and linked external material; DDN does not invent press coverage or reproduce other publications’ articles.

The selection is intentionally broad: graphic design can sit next to CG, painting, audiovisual performance, identity, fashion, film or something that does not fit neatly into a category.

Corrections, additions and removals can be handled through the publisher.

Publisher disclosure / DDN is an editorial project published by Archive42, Graz, Austria. When Archive42 itself is featured, that relationship is disclosed again inside the article.
