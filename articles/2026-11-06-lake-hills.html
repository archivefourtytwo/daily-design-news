Lake Hills

An audiovisual practice where electronic music, live performance and moving image are developed together.

Artist / DJ / ProducerLos Angeles, United StatesPrimary source ↗

Lake Hills sits in a part of electronic culture where sound and image are not separate deliverables.

His practice extends from music production into audiovisual performance and visual systems built for other artists.

The result is part producer, part visual artist and part live-performance world builder.
