Vincent Langaard

Pop mythology, western imagery and cultural symbols collide in Vincent Langaard’s image-heavy practice.

ArtistOslo, NorwayPrimary source ↗

Vincent Langaard’s catalogue moves through recurring visual worlds rather than a single neat series.

The work treats recognisable imagery as raw material, repeating and reframing familiar icons into a personal visual system.

His site groups the work into bodies including Liquid Metal, West World, Mythos and Reality Is Just a Collective Hunch.
