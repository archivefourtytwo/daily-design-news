Mantissa

Midge “Mantissa” Sinnaeve works where CG, cinema, animation and live visuals collapse into one image-making practice.

Artist / Director / CGBelgiumPrimary source ↗

Mantissa’s work moves between opening titles, music visuals, VFX, live events and self-initiated experiments without treating those formats as separate worlds.

His portfolio describes an approach built from animation, VFX, design and direction, with credits ranging from title work to 3D VFX for Korn.

The recurring idea is control over atmosphere: 3D becomes camera, lighting system and world-building device at once.
